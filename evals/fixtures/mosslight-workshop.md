# Mosslight Community Workshop

Mosslight is an invented community workshop used only for CiteNook evaluation. It is open Tuesday through Saturday from 09:30 to 17:30. It is closed on Sunday and Monday.

The safety induction begins every Thursday at 18:00, lasts 45 minutes, and accepts at most eight participants. Members must complete the induction before borrowing powered tools.

Each member may borrow no more than two tools at once. The standard loan period is seven calendar days. Battery-powered tools must be returned to the service desk by 16:30 on their due date.

The repair cafe runs on the first Saturday of each month from 10:00 to 12:00. Visitors may bring one broken household item, but the volunteers do not repair microwave ovens.

The rooftop rainwater tank holds 2,400 litres. Its water is used for the teaching garden and for cleaning outdoor tools; it is not drinking water.

An electric minibus stops at Elm Gate at 09:10 and 14:10 on workshop days. The ride is free, and bicycles may be carried only on the rear rack.

The materials cupboard sells reclaimed plywood sheets for three workshop tokens each. Members under eighteen do not pay a materials fee when attending a supervised school session.

The quiet design room is reservable in 30-minute blocks. A member may reserve at most two consecutive blocks, and food is not allowed in that room.
